## Observations that affect later phases

1. **Snapshots are disjoint (0 cross-step edges).** Time-based splits cannot leak
   through message passing. Confirms the design assumption (D5).
2. **Label prior shifts across the paper split** (11.6% → 6.5%). A threshold tuned
   on training steps will be miscalibrated on test; PR-AUC (threshold-free) must be
   reported alongside F1.
3. **Steps 43–46 have almost no illicit labels** (24, 24, 5, 2). Per-step F1 there is
   high-variance and should be shown with counts, not averaged silently.
4. **Strong covariate shift is concentrated in aggregated features** (top shifts:
   agg_8, agg_10, agg_7, with standardised mean differences above 3). Neighbour-
   aggregate features may generalise worse than local ones; Experiment 5 tests this.
5. **Illicit nodes have lower mean degree (2.01) than licit (3.10).** Topology alone
   carries some signal; the "topology only" ablation is worth running.
